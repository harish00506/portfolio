\begin{itemize}

  \item Scored out-of-sample on WAPE and MASE, with seasonal-naive as a mandatory baseline that ships

        of the data, and a GREEN / AMBER / RED certification reports that median WAPE is 16.6\% and

        requirement appears to be met.
\end{itemize}
