\section{Selected Projects}
\textbf{Demand Forecasting System} \textit{Python, PyCaret, scikit-learn, Docker, Streamlit}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Sole author. Monthly forecasts across roughly 1,500 product series from a tiered zoo of about
        150 labelled configurations, with one global model fitted across all series pooled rather
        than one per series, and a direct twelve-month horizon so error cannot compound.
  \item Scored out of sample on WAPE and MASE rather than MAPE, which explodes on zero months, with
        seasonal-naive as a mandatory baseline that ships if nothing beats it. Intervals are
        conformal, computed from model residuals rather than the variance of the data.
  \item A GREEN / AMBER / RED certification reports that median product-level WAPE is 16.6\% and that
        18.7\% of series land inside the client's requested 10\% band, rather than narrowing the
        bands until the requirement appears to be met.
\end{itemize}
